About

Gabe Marroquin is a multidisciplinary designer and self-taught visual artist based in Brooklyn, New York, where he is currently pursuing an MFA in Design and Technology at Parsons School of Design.

He specializes in visual identity, digital design, creative coding, and experimental artistic practices. He has worked with clients including the Daily Mail, Jackfir, and SolidSky.
